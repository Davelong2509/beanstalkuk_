# Careers at Beanstalk — implementation notes

## Source
- Supplied Beanstalk Website Mobile PDF, page 19.
- The supplied desktop PDF contains 18 pages and does not include a Careers page. The desktop composition is therefore a responsive adaptation of the mobile page, not a claimed desktop source match.

## Source copy retained
### Hero
- JOIN US
- Careers at Beanstalk.
- We are always glad to hear from kind, reliable people who love working with young children.

### What we look for
- People who put children first.
- Warmth and patience with babies, toddlers and young children
- Reliability and a team spirit
- A real interest in how children learn through play
- Respect for every family we work with

### Introduce yourself
- Send us a short note
- Tell us about you, your experience and the age group you enjoy working with. Current vacancies will be listed on this page.
- Email us

### Keeping children safe
- Safe recruitment.
- Keeping children safe is central to how we recruit. All appointments are subject to suitable pre-employment checks.

## Functional behaviour
- "Email us" opens the visitor's default email client.
- The recipient is the published nursery email address from `src/content/site.js`.
- The subject is prefilled as "Careers at Beanstalk Day Nursery".
- Careers is included in the Explore footer links.
- The page reuses the existing sunburst CTA.

## Content limitations
The source does not specify current vacancies, role descriptions, salaries, application deadlines, required qualifications, or a recruitment contact other than the nursery email. None are invented.
The desktop PDF ends at page 18, so the desktop grid layout is an adaptation of the mobile source.
