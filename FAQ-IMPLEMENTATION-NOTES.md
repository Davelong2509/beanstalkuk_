# Frequently Asked Questions — implementation notes

## Layout
- Hero title: "Frequently asked questions."
- Hero description: "Quick answers to common questions. If yours is not here, just ask."
- Narrow centred search and accordion column.
- Four category groups and nine question titles from the reference.
- "Still have a question? Come and ask us in person." visit/call strip.
- Shared dark-green animated CTA band: "Still have a question?"

## Exact categories and question titles
### Starting at Beanstalk
- What ages do you accept?
- How do I arrange a visit?
- How can I speak to the nursery team?

### Sessions, fees and funding
- Can I use funded childcare hours?
- How do I check if I am eligible?

### A day at Beanstalk
- How do you share my child's progress?

### Finding us and policies
- Where is the nursery located?
- Where can I read your policies?
- How do I make a complaint?

## Interactions
- Search matches question text, answer text, category labels and link labels.
- Clear-search button appears when there is a query.
- No-results message and clear-search action.
- One accordion item open at a time.
- Plus/minus control, aria-expanded, aria-controls and labelled answer region.
- Animated open/close; reduced-motion preference respected.
- Result count announced to screen readers.

## Answer content and limitations
The reference PDF displays only collapsed questions, not answer copy. FAQ answers are written from information already represented in the site's project content. Nursery management should approve all wording before production, especially the progress-sharing answer and the funding explanation. No session prices, opening hours, safeguarding contact names or undocumented progress-reporting methods are invented.
